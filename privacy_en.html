Chill Out It – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026　Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Policy is issued by the developer of Chill Out It (an individual developer, the "Developer") and explains how your personal information is handled. Please read it carefully before using the game.

1. Information the Developer Processes

As required by law, the Developer processes only the information necessary to provide the game's features.

The Developer does not collect or upload any of your personal information. The game does not require you to register or log in, and does not obtain your nickname, avatar, gender, region, phone number, ID number, or similar information. Game progress, settings and other data are stored only in the game cache on your device. The Developer has no servers of its own and cannot access this data.

Features such as vibration feedback and the sidebar revisit entry are implemented through interfaces provided by the Douyin platform and do not involve providing your personal information to the Developer.

2. Third-Party Plugins / SDKs

To provide certain features, the Developer may integrate plugins/SDKs provided by third parties. For how a third-party plugin/SDK handles personal information, please refer to the official notice published by that third party.

Chill Out It does not currently integrate any third-party plugin or SDK. If one is integrated in the future, the Developer will list the plugin/SDK name, the provider's name and the information it processes in this Policy, and will ask for your consent again.

3. Your Rights

  • The game does not request authorization to access your user information, phone number, location or similar data. If you have previously granted such permissions on the platform, you can withdraw them via: the "…" menu at the top right of the game's home screen → "Settings" → tap the relevant item → tap "Don't allow".
  • Clearing the game cache or deleting the game will delete the game data stored locally on your device.
  • You may contact the Developer as described in Section 7 to exercise your statutory rights to access, copy, correct or delete your personal information.

4. Storage of Information

Unless otherwise required by laws and regulations, the Developer will retain your information only for the shortest period necessary to fulfil the purpose of processing. The game's data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer or any third party.

5. Use of Information

  • The Developer will use collected information only for the purposes expressly stated in this Policy.
  • If the Developer intends to use your information beyond the purposes or reasonable scope of this Policy, the Developer will, before making such a change, notify you again via an in-game pop-up or an update to this Policy and obtain your express consent.

6. Disclosure of Information

  • The Developer will not proactively share or transfer your information to any third party. Where sharing or transfer is genuinely necessary, the Developer will obtain your separate consent directly or confirm that the third party has obtained it.
  • The Developer will not publicly disclose your information. Where public disclosure is required, the Developer will inform you of the purpose of the disclosure, the types of information disclosed and any information that may be involved, and obtain your separate consent.

7. Contact the Developer

If you believe the Developer has not complied with this Policy, or you have any other complaints or suggestions, or questions about the protection of minors' personal information, you may contact the Developer as follows. The Developer will respond within 15 working days.

8. Updates to this Policy

When this Policy is updated, the Developer will notify you in the game. For material changes (such as collecting new types of information or integrating a third-party SDK), the Developer will ask for your consent again.
